UNO Rules

Setup

  • 2 to 8 players
  • Each player draws 7 cards
  • Place the rest of the deck face down as the draw pile
  • Flip the top card to start the discard pile

How to Play

  • On your turn, play a card that matches the color, number, or symbol of the card on top of the discard pile
  • If you can't play, draw one card from the draw pile
  • If you can play the drawn card, you may play it immediately
  • Otherwise, your turn ends

Action Cards

Card Effect
Skip Next player loses their turn
Reverse Changes direction of play
Draw 2 Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn
Wild Changes the color to anything you choose
Wild Draw 4 Changes color AND next player draws 4 cards

Winning

  • Be the first player to get rid of all your cards
  • When you have 1 card left, you must say "UNO"
  • If you forget and someone catches you, you draw 2 penalty cards

Special Notes

  • If the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discard pile (except the top card) to make a new draw pile
  • You can play a Wild card even if you have other playable cards
  • Wild Draw 4 can only be played if you have no cards of the current color
